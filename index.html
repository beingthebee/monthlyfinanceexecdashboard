Exec Summary · May 2026
  • Orbitworks closed May with $20.03M cash (OW $9.69M · MLO $10.34M), representing a 14-month runway based on steady state burn rate of $1.5M/month and inflows from signed term sheets.
  • Headcount remained at 56 employees at end of May, against a budget of 68 FTE; key roles in Industrial Operations continue to be progressed.
  • Revenues recognised remain at $0.90M YTD. No additional revenue was recognised in May. First milestones from FADA-SIRB-1 and the FR contract are expected to be recorded in June.
  • OpEx remained below budget in May, driven by deferred IT Infrastructure spend (PLM/PDM licensing delayed to Q3), lower BD activity, and continued headcount ramp discipline. Power costs exceeded budget marginally due to higher facility usage and the Vibe table generator addition.
  • Marketing costs were higher in May due to MIITE 2026 event participation and related subscription spends. Freight & Logistics costs elevated due to US customs charges on TVAC system and Loft shipments.
